# Anargya team directory

Verified against the public [official Team page](https://www.anargyaits.com/team) on 10 October 2026. The page is labeled **8th Gen**; these records must not be presented as a verified ninth-generation or current roster.

## Method and coverage

All 65 published member-card references were downloaded temporarily outside the repository and visually inspected. Native Vision OCR assisted transcription; the final names and roles were checked against the card text. Grouping comes from the official page navigation and public page data, not guessed from portraits or filenames. Source roles retain their original uppercase wording. All 65 cards were readable: no unresolved name or role transcriptions.

**64 unique members, 65 role placements**, across four categories and 12 subdivisions. Putra Valen Simbolon is one person with two published roles: Mechanical Manager and Head of Vehicle Dynamics. Both placements are retained with one stable member identity and image path. Advisor Alief Wikarta S.T., M.Sc., Ph.D. is published separately and is not included in the 64-member count.

The source lists both Rasyad Afrizal Nuriman and Muchammad Alif Ismail Chakim as Heads of Tractive System & Controller, and both Fauzan Fairuzy Willis and Umarrahman Tajalli Zahrayudha as Heads of Electro Programming & Safety System. These are preserved without assigning an invented seniority. The Contact page identifies Annisa with a different managerial role; this directory follows the Team card (Staff Public Relations) and does not claim the different pages describe the same season. The current ninth-generation roster and simultaneous-role dates still require team confirmation.

Portrait research files remain under `/tmp/anargya-team-portraits/`, outside public assets and Git. None is used by the website. Every directory and advisor card uses the same neutral silhouette. Change a person's `imagePath` in `src/data/team-members.json` to replace it later; all their assignments share that image. The primary category/subdivision/role fields reflect the first published assignment; `assignments` preserves every placement and its evidence URL.

## Page content and art direction

History uses the [official About page](https://www.anargyaits.com/about-us): September 2018 establishment, 2023 battery/chassis development, and 2024 Mark 5.0 aerodynamics/telemetry/vehicle dynamics. Existing official group and engineering photographs are reused with context-preserving captions; no portraits were added to public assets. Division overviews list the official subdivision names rather than invented responsibility descriptions.

[Sapu Angin Team](https://www.itsteamsapuangin.com/team) informs the photographic opening, history narrative, organizational introduction, and division-based directory only. Anargya's black, green, white, typography tokens and 6px corners remain authoritative.
