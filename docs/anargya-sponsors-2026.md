## Media-partner investigation

The July reports name Brigade Pasopati, AIESEC Surabaya, YouthBreakBoundaries, CNN, AnakTeknikIndonesia, Kultur Domestik, Lambe Resing, Cardrive, Autodoze, Indozone, Seven Events, Dyandra Promosindo, IIMS, KOLEKSI and GNFI: 15 unique reported media/event/community names, distinct from the 25 supporter list.

The official team sponsor wall and existing manifest do not provide a separate matching media group. Focused searches found the same related reports and Brigade Pasopati’s actual team coverage, but did not establish team-side season approval for this complete media roster. Coverage or event participation alone is not a media-partner agreement. No media group or media-logo substitutions are added to the homepage pending confirmation. All 15 remain report-only records; they are not counted in the 27 imported supporter logos.

## Asset and usage status

27 original raster logos available; none missing in the selected supporter inventory. Twenty-five existing files were reused. IKOMA’s original PNG was retrieved from its official website; Pertamina NRE’s original 3682×872 PNG was recovered unchanged from the inline logo SVG on its official homepage, replacing the padded team-hosted version for readability. No unrelated logo, synthetic wordmark, or text approximation is used. Original PNGs remain unmodified. Explicit reuse licenses were not established; publication permissions remain a review item. Some assets use a light neutral backing inside the dark tile to keep original dark lettering legible; colors and aspect ratios are preserved.
